An Illustrated Astronomy Book · Vol. 1

Your Gold Did Not Come From Earth

Hold a plain gold ring up to the light and ask where the metal came from. No star can make it. This book follows the atoms from the crash of two dead stars to the ring on your finger, and says plainly which parts are still debated.
